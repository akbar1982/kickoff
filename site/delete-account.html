Delete your Kickoff account

This page explains how to delete your Kickoff account and the data linked to it.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kickoff and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. If you host upcoming games that players have already paid for, cancel and refund those first.

Without the app

If you can't use the app, email akbar.yuldoshev.professional@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Kickoff account" from the email address on your account. We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile (name, email, photo, sign-in details)Deleted
RSVPs, waitlist spots and poll votesDeleted
Chat messages you postedDeleted
Group membershipsDeleted. If you're a group's last organizer, its longest-standing member becomes organizer; a group with no other members is deleted.
Games you hostDeleted, including their chats
Kickoff's record of your paymentsDeleted
Payment records held by StripeKept by Stripe and the organizer as required for tax and fraud prevention (typically up to 7 years). See Stripe's privacy policy.
Organizer's Stripe accountKickoff unlinks it. The Stripe account itself is yours; close it at dashboard.stripe.com.

Deleted data is removed from our backups within 30 days. Deletion can't be undone.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete your own chat messages by long-pressing them, leave groups and games at any time, and edit your profile in the app. For anything else, email akbar.yuldoshev.professional@gmail.com.
