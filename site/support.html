Common questions

How do I join a game?

Open a game from the Games or Map tab and tap Join. If the game is full, you're added to the waitlist and moved in automatically when a spot opens.

I paid but I'm not shown as going

Payments usually confirm within a few seconds. Pull down to refresh the game. If you're still not shown as going after a few minutes, email us with the game name. Your card is only charged once.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the game's organizer. Message them in the game chat. If a game was cancelled and you can't reach the organizer, email us.

How do I organize a game and get paid?

Tap + on the Games tab to post a game. For paid games you can either collect payments in the app (set up payouts on the Me tab through Stripe; Stripe's fees are paid by you) or ask players to pay you directly and mark them paid yourself.

How do I create or join a group?

Go to the Groups tab. Anyone can create a group and becomes its organizer. You can join a group by invitation or by requesting to join and waiting for an organizer to approve you.

Someone is being abusive. What do I do?

Long-press one of their messages and choose Report or Block. You can unblock people on the Me tab under Blocked users. We review reports within 24 hours. If someone's safety is at risk, contact local emergency services first.
