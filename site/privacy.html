Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Kickoff ("Kickoff", "we", "us") is a mobile app operated by Akbar Yuldoshev for finding, organizing and paying for pickup soccer games. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email akbar.yuldoshev.professional@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), display name and, if you sign in with Google, Facebook or Apple, the name, email and profile photo that service shares with us.
  • Games and groups: games you create (title, description, venue name, address and map location, date, time, price, player limits and payment instructions), groups you create or join, your role in them, and your RSVPs and poll votes.
  • Messages: messages you post in the community chat, group chats and game chats.
  • Organizer payout details: if you collect payments in the app, you set up a Stripe account. You give your identity, tax and bank details directly to Stripe, not to us. We only keep your Stripe account ID and whether payments and payouts are enabled.

Information collected when you use the app

  • Location: with your permission, the app uses your device's location to show games near you and center the map. Your location is used on your device and is not stored on our servers. You can turn location off at any time in your device settings and still use the app.
  • Payment records: when you pay for a game in the app, we record the game, the amount, the currency, the status (for example "succeeded" or "refunded") and Stripe's payment reference. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach our servers.
  • Technical data: our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, time and the request made) to operate and secure the service.

We do not use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: create your account, show games and groups, manage RSVPs, waitlists and chats.
  • To process payments for games and keep organizers' payout status up to date.
  • To keep Kickoff safe: investigate reports, remove content that breaks our Terms, and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To contact you about your account, for example password resets and important service notices.

What other users can see

  • Your display name and profile photo are visible to other Kickoff users.
  • Games marked "Open to everyone" are visible to all users. Group-only games, group chats and members lists are visible to that group's members.
  • Messages you post are visible to everyone in that chat.
  • Game hosts and group organizers can see who is going, who is on the waitlist and who has paid.

When you pay for a game in the app, the payment is made to the organizer's own Stripe account, so the organizer receives your name or email and the payment details that Stripe shows to merchants. When a game uses "pay the organizer directly", you pay the organizer outside Kickoff (for example by Venmo, Zelle or cash), and that payment is governed by the service you use.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of our users or the public, or as part of a merger or sale of the service, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, RSVPs, votes, chat messages, group memberships and the games you host within 30 days. Payment records held by Stripe, and any we must keep for tax, accounting or legal reasons, may be kept for longer as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your profile in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete your account and data at any time. See how to delete your account.
  • Location: you can turn location access on or off in your device settings.
  • Other requests: depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have the right to request a copy of your data, object to certain uses, or complain to a data protection authority. Email akbar.yuldoshev.professional@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to it is restricted by database security rules, and payment details are handled only by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Kickoff is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
